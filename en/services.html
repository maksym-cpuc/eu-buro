Visas and residency

Visas, residency and relocation to Europe and the USA

In Europe, we support the journey from your first residence permit to your citizenship application. For the USA, we help you navigate visa and immigration matters. We agree on the destination and scope of support after assessing your individual circumstances.

Get a free residency assessment

What we do

Stage 1

Choosing your residency route

We compare countries and residency routes for your circumstances.

  • Residence permits for remote workers
  • Residence permits based on passive income
  • Study, family and business routes
  • Income requirement assessment
Stage 2

Qualifying residence

We help maintain continuous residence for the required period.

  • Obtaining your residence card
  • Renewals to maintain your status
  • Changing your residency route
  • Tracking deadlines and absences
Stage 3

Citizenship documents

We assemble a complete application to meet the country's requirements.

  • Proof of residence
  • Income and tax records
  • Certificates and legalisation
  • Checks before submission
Stage 4

Exams

We help you prepare for the exams required by the country.

  • Reaching the required language level
  • Knowledge of local history and laws
  • Exam registration
  • A preparation plan
Stage 5

Citizenship application

We submit your application and follow it through to a decision.

  • Application and appointment
  • Responses to the authority's requests
  • Application tracking
  • Oath and passport
