What we do

We help with visas, residency and immigration matters in Europe and the USA. We review other destinations and unusual situations individually.

  1. Choosing your residency route

    We compare countries and residency routes against your income, work and family circumstances, choosing the option with the strongest legal basis.

  2. Qualifying residence

    We help with permits, timely renewals and changes of residency route, keeping track of absences that could interrupt your qualifying residence.

  3. Citizenship documents

    We assemble evidence of residence, income, taxes and integration, and arrange translations and legalisation.

  4. Exams

    We help you prepare for language and civic knowledge exams: the level required, where to take them and how to prepare.

  5. Citizenship application

    We submit your application, respond to requests from the authority and follow your case through to a decision.

Our principles

Lawful routes only

Our strategies are based on lawful residence, helping clients live in Europe legally from day one.

An honest assessment

If your income or documents do not yet meet the requirements, we tell you on the first call, not months later.

Official sources

We check requirements and timelines against each country's legislation and official guidance.

Transparent fees

Stages, documents and timelines are clear before we start. Fees are paid in stages, without full payment upfront.

The authority makes the decision

The government makes the final decision. We take responsibility for careful preparation: the correct legal basis, complete documents and accurate translations.

Confidentiality

Your passport, financial and family information is used only for your case.
