Five steps to citizenship

Join us at any stage, whether you are just starting out or already living in Europe and preparing to apply for citizenship.

Discuss my situation

Worried you might not qualify?

We assess your situation and look for a lawful route in a country whose requirements you meet. The authority makes the final decision, so we do not promise approval. We take responsibility for the quality of our work.

A complex case? A previous refusal, income that is hard to document or no travel history: these are reasons to discuss your situation, not to give up.

“I don't know which country suits me”

We compare income requirements, time to citizenship, language and taxes. We show you two or three realistic options instead of a one-size-fits-all answer.

“I have income, but it is hard to prove”

We work with self-employed clients and income from several sources, organising contracts, statements and tax returns to meet the authority's requirements.

“I'm worried about losing my qualifying residence”

Long absences and missed renewals can disrupt qualifying residence. We keep track of your status and deadlines and remind you in advance.

“I'm worried about the citizenship exam”

We explain the language level required in your country, where to take the exam and how to prepare without unnecessary expense.

“I've had debts or a criminal conviction”

We review what will appear in your records and how the country's rules apply, then identify a country and route with a lower risk.

“I'm nervous about paying remotely”

Frequently asked questions

How much does your support cost?

It depends on the country and stage of your case. We provide an exact quote after a free assessment, with payments made in stages.

How long does it take to qualify for citizenship?

Many European countries require five to ten years of legal residence. The period, language and integration requirements vary by country. We assess the timeline for your circumstances during the consultation.

Do I need to speak the local language?

It is often not required for a residence permit. Citizenship usually requires a language test and sometimes a civic knowledge test. We explain the required level and help you prepare.

Can the process be handled online?

Yes. Consultations, agreements, document preparation and communication are handled online. You only need to attend in person where the authority requires it.

Which languages are consultations available in?

We speak with clients in Russian or Spanish and communicate with authorities in the language of the country of application.
